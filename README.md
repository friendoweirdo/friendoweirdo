## About Mintberry Crunch

Mintberry Crunch is the superhero alter-ego of Bradley Biggle, a boy with mint and berry powers. Originally seen as a goofy member of Coon and Friends, he later proves himself heroic, using his abilities in cosmic-scale adventures.
